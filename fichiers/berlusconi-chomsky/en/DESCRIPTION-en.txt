Silvio Berlusconi face à Noam Chomsky — The right's takeover of the media in a heated debate

A media magnate turned right-wing Prime Minister faces a media critic and propaganda theorist. Two visions of the world clash in a fiery debate about the rightward drift of the media and the rise of the extreme right globally. The entire debate is in French, with the translator's voice overlaid — like the great international interviews. From Berlusconi's Italy to France's CNews and Bolloré, passing through Fox News in the United States, this portrait explores how media concentration has transformed the global political landscape.
