René Quinton — The Man Who Discovered We Are Fragments of the Ocean

René Quinton (1866–1925) was a French biologist, physiologist, and pioneer of military aviation. He is best known for discovering marine plasma — the remarkable similarity between seawater and human blood — and for developing injectable seawater treatments that saved countless lives.

In this episode, we explore the extraordinary life of this man who dared to inject seawater into gravely ill patients at a time when the medical establishment ridiculed him. From his laboratory experiments to his marine plasma clinics, and from his role in French aviation before World War I to his lasting legacy in alternative medicine, René Quinton is a figure unlike any other — at the crossroads of science, medicine, and adventure.

Marine plasma, this diluted seawater product, has become a recognized health product worldwide. But beyond its medical applications, it is the story of a man who followed his scientific intuition when everyone told him he was wrong.
