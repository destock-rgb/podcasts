René Quinton — Children of the Sea

What if your blood was nothing but ancient seawater, preserved in your veins for three billion years? French biologist René Quinton proved exactly that — by draining a dog of all its blood and replacing it with filtered ocean water. The dog lived.

In this episode, we meet the self-taught genius who stunned the scientific establishment, saved thousands of infants from cholera with nothing but seawater, co-founded French aviation, and then vanished from history. From the Academy of Sciences to the maternity wards of Paris, from the Atlantic Ocean to the skies above France, Quinton's story is one of the most extraordinary — and most forgotten — in modern science. A conversation about the sea inside us, the courage to defy the establishment, and the patience of the ocean.
