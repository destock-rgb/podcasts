Don Miguel Ruiz — The Four Agreements
A Mexican surgeon falls asleep at the wheel and survives a crash that should have killed him. He walks away from medicine — and writes one of the most widely read spiritual books of all time.

In this documentary portrait, we follow Miguel Ángel Ruiz from his mother's healing house in Guadalajara to the operating room, from the highway accident that changed his life to his return to his family's Toltec tradition. We walk through the four agreements one by one — be impeccable with your word, don't take anything personally, don't make assumptions, always do your best — with a warm but critical eye: who were the real Toltecs of Tula, what the modern "Toltec" revival owes to the twentieth century, and why psychologists still find echoes of cognitive therapy in these four simple sentences.
