Alfred Tomatis — The Man Who Taught Us to Listen

What if your ear did much more than just hear? What if it powered your brain, your voice, your very energy? French physician Alfred Tomatis spent a lifetime proving exactly that — and his controversial method is still practiced worldwide.

In this episode, we sit down with the inventor of the "Tomatis effect" — the discovery that the voice can only produce what the ear can perceive. From the roar of aircraft factories to the hushed halls of Benedictine monasteries, from Mozart's high frequencies to the filtered voice of a mother heard from the womb, Tomatis reveals how sound shapes us from our very first moments. A conversation about listening, healing, and the three laws that changed our understanding of the human ear.
