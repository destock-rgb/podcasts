Michel Foucault Facing Youth Violence

A fictional podcast episode imagining a conversation with Michel Foucault on youth violence in France today.
The philosopher corrects misquotations and refuses both to glorify violence and to demonize the young.

This episode traces Foucault's intellectual trajectory from Histoire de la folie (1961) through Surveiller et punir (1975), his years at the Sorbonne and the College de France (1970-1984), and his involvement with the GIPU and the Service d'action ducative. Foucault dismantles the myth of the philosopher who only cared about power, corrects the abusive citation "the prison produces delinquency," and explains how dispositifs, biopower, and governmentality shape the lives of young people.
