Martin Luther King Facing Racial Inequality Today

A fictional radio portrait of Dr. King, revisiting the defining moments of the civil rights movement from the inside.
The dialogue is a fictional reconstruction, not a historical recording.

This episode traces King's journey from the "I Have a Dream" speech at the Lincoln Memorial to the Poor People's Campaign, examining nonviolent leadership, the struggle for economic justice, and the unfinished work of racial equality. King corrects the record on key details, including the true name of the 1963 march and the origin of his most famous words, while offering a deeply personal meditation on legacy, sacrifice, and the ongoing fight for justice.
